  • ⏱️

    Strict 15-Minute Timer

    The countdown timer starts the moment you click "Start Quiz". If the timer reaches 00:00, your quiz will automatically be submitted with your current selections.

  • ⚠️

    Tab Switching Detection

    Tab switching is actively detected and recorded. Navigating away from the quiz tab to other browser tabs or windows will trigger a violation counter and prompt a warning alert.

  • 🔄

    Leaving / Refreshing Warning

    Reloading the page, closing the tab, or navigating backward will trigger a browser warning. Please avoid refreshing during your test attempt.

  • 🔒

    One-Time Submission

    Once you click "Submit Quiz" and confirm, your attempt is finalized and completed. You will not be able to re-take or modify answers.

  • 🧭

    Question Navigation

    You can freely navigate between all 20 questions using the Previous/Next buttons or the question grid palette. Only one option (A, B, C, or D) can be chosen per question.
